Role Definitions

Official Coffee Beats job descriptions with measurable KPI-linked outcomes

📍 Maadi Branch · Specialty Coffee for Creatives
🎯 Role Overview

Head Barista

The creative and technical leader of the bar. At Coffee Beats, the Head Barista is not just a coffee maker — they are a cultural curator who sets the standard for every cup, every interaction, and every atmosphere behind the counter. They own the craft, coach the team, and safeguard the brand's commitment to consistency, quality, and warmth.

Leadership Operations Full-Time
📊 Reports To / Manages
Reports to
Branch Manager · Founder
Manages
All Baristas on shift
KPI Target
≥ 85 / 100
✅ Key Responsibilities & Measurable Outcomes

⭐ Coffee Quality & Consistency

  • Maintain espresso extraction parameters (18–21g dose, 25–32s pull, 1:2 ratio) on every shift KPI: Consistency Score ≥ 90%
  • Conduct morning calibration before opening — dial in grinder, check milk temperatures, taste each brew method of the day
  • Perform weekly blind consistency checks across the team (taste test, compare against standard recipe card)
  • Zero tolerance for serving out-of-spec drinks — empower team to remake without hesitation KPI: Drink Remake Rate ≤ 2%
  • Introduce one new seasonal drink or technique per quarter

👥 Team Leadership & Coaching

  • Coach baristas through daily side-by-side feedback, not written reports KPI: Team Avg Score ≥ 75
  • Complete one structured coaching session per barista per month (documented on scorecard)
  • Onboard every new hire in their first 2 weeks using the Coffee Beats training protocol
  • Identify top performers each month and nominate them for the reward system
  • Run a 10-minute team huddle at the start of every opening shift (menu, events, goals)

⏱️ Punctuality & Attendance

  • Arrive 15 minutes before shift start for equipment warm-up and team handover KPI: Late Arrivals ≤ 1/month
  • Maintain ≥ 95% attendance — covers are arranged proactively, not last-minute KPI: Attendance ≥ 95%
  • Complete and submit daily shift log before end of shift

♻️ Waste & Cost Control

  • Own waste tracking for the bar — log all waste daily in this ops system KPI: Waste ≤ target/month
  • Brief team on waste targets at start of every shift
  • Monitor milk, bean, and syrup par levels daily; escalate supply issues proactively KPI: Zero stockouts caused by failure to escalate
  • Monthly waste analysis — present trends and action plan to management

🧹 Cleanliness & Station Standards

  • Complete opening and closing cleanliness checklists — verified daily KPI: Cleanliness ≥ 90%
  • Enforce FIFO on all perishables — no expired product ever serves a customer
  • Ensure espresso machine, grinder, steam wands are cleaned to standard each shift
  • Conduct monthly deep-clean drill with the team

📈 Upselling & Revenue Contribution

  • Achieve average ticket size target as set per month KPI: Avg Ticket ≥ EGP target
  • Lead by example in recommending add-ons, seasonal items, and experience upgrades
  • Review own and team upsell metrics from sales reports uploaded monthly

😊 Customer Experience

  • Ensure every customer interaction reflects Coffee Beats' warmth and cultural identity KPI: Customer Score ≥ 4.3/5
  • Personally resolve any escalated complaints within 5 minutes, with a solution — not an apology alone
  • Curate the bar's sensory experience (music, aroma, layout) for each session type

🎯 Role Overview

Barista

The soul of the bar. At Coffee Beats, every barista is a creative professional who happens to make incredible coffee. You're making drinks for filmmakers, musicians, and artists — your consistency, warmth, and craft are part of the experience they came for. You own your station, your craft, and your customer relationship.

Craft Customer Full-Time / Part-Time
📊 Reports To / Collaborates
Reports to
Head Barista · Branch Manager
Collaborates with
All team members on shift
KPI Target
≥ 75 / 100
✅ Key Responsibilities & Measurable Outcomes

☕ Coffee Craft

  • Prepare all drinks according to Coffee Beats recipe cards — every time, not most times KPI: Consistency ≥ 85%
  • Follow calibration set by Head Barista at shift start — no freelancing on ratios
  • Complete milk texturing, latte art, and pour technique training milestones on schedule
  • Remake any drink that doesn't meet standard without being asked KPI: Remake Rate ≤ 3%

⏱️ Punctuality & Attendance

  • Arrive on time or early — late arrivals directly impact team prep and customer experience KPI: Late Arrivals ≤ 2/month
  • Maintain ≥ 90% attendance, arrange cover proactively with Head Barista when unavoidable
  • Complete daily shift log before clocking out KPI: Log Completion ≥ 90%

♻️ Waste Control

  • Self-report all waste honestly at end of every shift — honesty is rewarded, hiding is not KPI: Shots ≤ 3/shift, Milk ≤ 100ml/shift
  • Improve waste numbers month-over-month as a growth goal
  • Suggest waste reduction techniques in team huddles

🧹 Station & Cleanliness

  • Complete daily cleanliness checklist — opening and/or closing KPI: Cleanliness ≥ 85%
  • Steam wand wiped and purged after every single use — non-negotiable
  • Keep bar counter clear and visually attractive during service at all times

📈 Upselling & Menu Knowledge

  • Know the full menu — ingredients, allergens, and the story behind each drink KPI: Menu Quiz ≥ 80%
  • Naturally recommend add-ons and seasonal specials to every customer KPI: Avg Ticket growth
  • Mention at least one pairing suggestion per order during activation events

😊 Customer Interaction

  • Greet every customer with warmth and eye contact — no heads-down bar culture here KPI: Customer Score ≥ 4.0/5
  • Remember regulars' names and orders — this is the Coffee Beats difference
  • Escalate complaints to Head Barista immediately — never dismiss or minimize

🌱 Growth & Learning

  • Complete one training module or technique per month KPI: Training Progress
  • Participate in monthly team tastings and calibration sessions
  • Set one personal growth goal each month and review it with Head Barista
